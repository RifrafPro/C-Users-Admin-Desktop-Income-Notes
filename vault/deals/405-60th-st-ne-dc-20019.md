### 2. Zoning (Play A gate)
- **Zone: RA-1** (ZR58 equivalent R-5-A). Source: DCOZ Zone_Mapservice layer 24 point query at the lot centroid (-76.914627, 38.89493), 2026-10-12. MLS also shows RA-1. **Not R-2/R-3**, so the single-family subdivision rules (R-2 4,000 sf minimum) do NOT apply here. [Certain]
- No historic district on the lot (layer 6 returned zero features). [Certain]
- RA-1 matter-of-right standards, read directly from 11-F DCMR (Subtitle F, dcoz.dc.gov PDF):
  - **FAR 0.9** (§302.1). 1.08 only for *voluntary* IZ, which needs a BZA special exception (§302.3/§5206.1)
  - Height **40 ft / 3 stories** (§303.1) · lot occupancy **40%** (§304.1) · rear yard **20 ft** (§305.1) · GAR 0.4
  - Side yards: **8 ft** for a detached/semi-detached building with 1–2 units. None for a row building with 1–2 units (§306.1). Multi-unit buildings: 3 in./ft of height, min 8 ft, one or two sides (§306.2)
  - **Row dwellings: 1,800 sf of land each** (§201.2). That is the only lot-area minimum written in Subtitle F
  - DCOZ "Maximum Number of Units" layer for this spot: **"Multiple Units", IZ Applicable** [Certain]
- **What 9,760 sf can yield** (my arithmetic, not an architect's test fit):
  - **Max gross floor area by right:** 9,760 × 0.9 = **8,784 sf**. Footprint cap 9,760 × 40% = 3,904 sf. [Certain] arithmetic
  - **2 lots (subdivide):** about 32–37 ft × 142 ft ≈ 4,880 sf each. Feasible on paper. A detached house on a 32–37 ft lot with 8-ft side yards is only 16–21 ft wide, so a **semi-detached pair** fits better. [Likely]
  - **Rowhouses:** land allows 5 (9,760/1,800). Frontage is the binding limit. The end units are semi-detached and need 8 ft side yards, so on a 63–74 ft frontage, **3 rowhouses** (about 16–19 ft wide each) is realistic and 4 is very tight (12–14.5 ft). [Likely]
  - **Small apartment house:** about 8,784 sf GFA → roughly **9 units**, which stays under the 10-unit IZ trigger. Multi-unit is shown as permitted on the DCOZ map layer. [Likely] Confirm the Subtitle U ch. 4 use table and the Subtitle C ch. 3 subdivision rules with a DC zoning attorney or architect before relying on this
- **Implication:** density is the real value here, not luxury SFH land. The buyer pool is DC infill/rental developers and BRRRR/flip investors. Luxury builders are not in it.
