# Income Notes — Business Workflow Map (v1)

Note-investing pipeline (secondary to FRESH GROUND wholesaling). Turn a lead into a
closed, serviced, logged note position, then exit for income or profit.

> NOT legal/financial advice. [Pro] stages need a licensed professional (attorney,
> licensed servicer, title co., CPA). Heavily regulated (Dodd-Frank/SAFE Act,
> RESPA/TILA, state foreclosure + usury). Route to pros, don't replace them.

## Deal types
- A. Buy existing notes — performing (income) / non-performing (workout/rehab).
- B. Create notes — seller-finance a property, hold or sell the paper.
- C. Flip paper — assign/double-close a note for a spread.

## Pipeline (stage · gate · fill)
1. Lead sourcing (brokers, marketplaces e.g. Paperstac, iSpeedToLead) → `lead-ingest`
2. Intake & triage (Deal Record + buy-box) → `deal-intake`
3. Underwriting — collateral · lien position · performance · borrower; ITV/LTV/yield → `note-analyzer`
4. Valuation & LOI (max bid from target yield/ITV) → note-analyzer + LOI draft
5. Due diligence [Pro] — collateral file (note, mortgage/DOT, allonge, assignment
   chain, title/O&E), liens, taxes, insurance, occupancy; BPO → `dd-checklist`
6. Closing [Pro] — PSA, endorsement (allonge) + assignment, fund, record, servicing
   transfer, collateral custody
7. Servicing [Pro] — board w/ licensed servicer; performing=collect; NPN=workout
   (mod/forbearance/DIL/foreclosure) or rehab to re-performing → `asset-monitor`
8. Exit — hold / partial / sell RPN at markup / foreclose→REO / wholesale note
9. P&L & compliance → `portfolio-ledger`

## Buy-Box (Rich to set numbers)
1st vs 2nd; performing/NPN/RPN; max ITV (~65–70%); min equity%; min yield/IRR;
states (judicial vs non-judicial); price band; min seasoning; max check size.
