# FRESH GROUND — Situational Script Library (companion to the 10-script playbook)
*(Built 2026-10-16 on Rich's order: "a script for every reason a distressed property sells,
and for every reason a buyer wants it." Style matches the playbook: dense, not verbatim.
Scripts number 11+. The voice agent may run DISCOVERY from these; price/offer talk stays
with Rich until carve-out #1 is activated.)*

## UNIVERSAL RULES (apply to every script below — non-negotiable)
- **Identity:** Rich Fabiani, d/b/a FRESH GROUND — "I buy properties for cash / connect
  them with builders and investors." NEVER "agent," "broker," "advisor," "counselor."
  AI caller discloses it's an AI in the first breath (KB §1).
- **Honest-lowball framing (Fullmer):** "My offer will be below retail — that's the trade
  for cash, as-is, and certainty. If top dollar matters more than speed, list it with an
  agent, and I'll tell you that to your face."
- **The two magic questions live in EVERY seller script:** "What's got you thinking about
  selling now?" · "Why do you think it hasn't sold / what's kept you from selling?"
- **Hard stops (KB §2):** legal Qs, tax Qs, "can I even sell this?" → STOP → Rich. Seller
  in crisis/grief → slow down, human first. "Stop calling" → permanent opt-out, all channels.
- **Never promise:** to stop a foreclosure, fix their credit, guarantee a date, or that
  they "have no other options." The honest alternative list (list it / loan mod / keep it)
  stated out loud is our best credibility weapon AND our legal shield.
- **Silence after the question.** The pause is the tool.

# PART A — SELLER SCRIPTS BY SITUATION

## 11 — Pre-Foreclosure / Behind on Payments ⚠️ LEGAL GATE
⚠️ **MD: PHIFA (§7-301+) regulates buying from owners in default — special contract,
rescission rights, penalties. VA/DC have their own foreclosure-rescue rules. STANDING
GATE: no contract with an owner in default until the flat-fee attorney review (assessment
10-15 §3.4) is done. Scripts below are for CONVERSATION ONLY until then.**
- Opener: "I'm not a bank and I'm not a rescue company — I buy houses for cash. I don't
  know your situation, but if selling before the auction is on your list of options, I
  can tell you in 48 hours exactly what I'd pay."
- Discovery: How far along is it (notice? sale date?) · what's owed vs. what it's worth ·
  lender been in contact? · "What would a good outcome look like for YOU — stay, sell, walk?"
- Honest frame: "Selling to me beats an auction only if the math leaves you walking away
  with something. Let's find out the number before you decide anything."
- Objections: "A company said they'd save my house" → "Be careful — ask them for the fee
  in writing. I'm simpler: I buy it or I don't." · "I'm getting a loan mod" → "Then take
  it — and keep my number in case it stalls. No hard feelings either way."
- Voicemail: name + "cash buyer, not a rescue outfit — if a sale before the deadline is
  one of your options, I can give you a real number fast." Number 2x.

## 12 — Tax-Delinquent / Tax Lien
- Opener: "County records show the taxes on [property] have fallen behind — that's public,
  it's how I found you, and it usually means the property's become a burden."
- Discovery: How many years behind · any payment plan with the county · is the property
  worth keeping to you · anything else owed on it (the mess = the margin).
- Key line: "Back taxes come out of the price at closing — you don't write a check, the
  title company handles it. You walk away with the difference, clean."
- Objection "lien's too big": "Let's math it together — if the number still leaves you
  something, good. If it doesn't, I'll tell you straight and you've lost ten minutes."
- ⚠️ Know the county's redemption timeline before calling (urgency honesty, not pressure).

## 13 — Rundown / Deferred Maintenance ("I can't afford to fix it")
- Opener: "I buy houses exactly as they sit — I've bought them with roofs gone and
  kitchens from 1964. You fix nothing, clean nothing, apologize for nothing."
- Discovery: What would YOU fix first if money were no object (reveals the pain list) ·
  lived-in or vacant · any systems dead (heat, water, roof) · what's the dream exit?
- Key line: "Every contractor quote you've gotten is my problem to carry, not yours.
  That's exactly what the price discount buys you — zero hassle."
- Objection "it just needs a little work": "Maybe! If an agent can get you more after
  repairs and commissions, I'll say so. Want me to give you both numbers?"

## 14 — Code Violations / County Citations / Condemnation Threat
- Opener: "Those citation letters don't stop — I take the property AND the violations.
  The county starts chasing me instead of you."
- Discovery: What's cited · fines accruing? · court date? · can you keep up with it?
- Key line: "The day we close, the letters become my letters. That's the product I sell."
- ⚠️ Never advise them to ignore a citation or miss a hearing (that's legal advice). If
  there's a court date, STOP → Rich.

## 15 — Hoarder House (dignity script)
- RULE: never say hoarder, mess, filth, or "state of the house." Say "full."
- Opener: "I buy houses with everything still in them. Take the photo albums and whatever
  matters — I handle every single thing that's left, and nobody walks through judging."
- Key differentiator: NO showings, NO strangers touring, NO cleanout demanded. For this
  seller, privacy is worth more than price — offer it loudly, honestly.
- Discovery: Anyone living there · how long since it felt manageable · what would leaving
  look like if it were easy?
- Pace: slowest script in the library. Two calls before any number is normal.

## 16 — Fire / Water / Storm Damage (insurance fatigue)
- Opener: "Insurance done paying but the house still isn't whole? I buy fire and water
  houses as they stand — mid-repair, tarp on the roof, doesn't matter."
- Discovery: Claim settled or open (⚠️ open claim = title company handles assignment Qs —
  STOP on specifics) · living where meanwhile · contractor quotes so far · structural?
- Key line: "You've been living the gap between what insurance paid and what repair
  costs. My price closes that gap TODAY instead of in a year of contractors."

## 17 — Divorce (neutrality script)
- RULE: absolute neutrality. Never "your ex," never agree with blame, never relay
  messages between spouses. If both are on title, BOTH sign — say so early and plainly.
- Opener: "A fast, clean, no-drama sale where neither side has to host open houses or
  agree on a realtor — that's the one thing I'm good for."
- Key line: "Cash, one closing date, each side's share wired separately by the title
  company. Nobody has to trust anybody."
- Discovery: Both on title? · attorneys involved (if court controls the asset → STOP →
  Rich) · timeline driver (support orders, new homes)?

## 18 — Tired Landlord / Problem Tenants
- Opener: "I buy rentals WITH the tenants in them — paying, not paying, mid-eviction,
  doesn't matter. The day we close, the 2am calls ring my phone."
- Discovery: Occupied/paying? · lease or month-to-month · eviction filed? (⚠️ never advise
  on eviction strategy — STOP) · how long have you owned it · "What finally made you done?"
- Key line: "You've done the math on one more turnover, one more roof, one more vacancy.
  I'm the number that ends the spreadsheet."

## 19 — Inherited-but-Stuck (pre-probate confusion — distinct from 05)
- Use when: owner died, family hasn't opened probate / doesn't know what's required.
- Opener: "Families sit on these houses for years because nobody knows step one. I work
  with estates constantly — the title company maps what the estate needs; costs you nothing
  to find out where you stand."
- ⚠️ WE never explain probate law. Line: "That's exactly what the title company's attorney
  answers for free when we open a file — want me to set that up?"
- **Heir-silo protocol (Fullmer):** multiple heirs = separate conversations, never a
  family meeting, never relay one heir's words to another.

## 20 — Senior Downsizing / Assisted-Living Transition (extra-care script)
- RULE: slowest sale wins. INVITE the family: "Is there a son, daughter, or friend you'd
  want on our next call? I prefer it." (Protects them AND us.)
- Opener: "Moves like this have enough stress without showings and repair lists. I buy
  as-is, you pick the closing date — even months out — and you take only what you love."
- Key offers: rent-back ("sell now, stay till the room is ready") · leave-everything ·
  close timed to the facility date.
- ⚠️ Any sign of confusion about what's being discussed → pause everything → Rich, family
  channel only. We walk away from any deal the seller doesn't fully track.

## 21 — Job Loss / Medical / Relocation (life-event script)
- Opener: "When life moves faster than a listing can, that's the gap I fill. Thirty days,
  cash, done — and if you have more time than that, I'll tell you listing might net more."
- Discovery: Hard date? · payments current (if behind → script 11 rules) · equity sense ·
  family aligned?
- Key line: "You're not selling a house, you're buying speed. Price reflects that trade —
  I'll show you the math in the open."

## 22 — Bankruptcy ⚠️ LEGAL GATE
- ⚠️ In an ACTIVE bankruptcy the trustee/court controls the asset. One discovery question
  only: "Has it been filed, and which chapter?" If filed → "Your attorney/trustee has to
  bless any sale — have them call me." STOP → Rich. No price talk. Ever.

## 23 — Liens & Judgments (mechanic's, HOA, judgments)
- Opener: "Liens don't scare me — they're a payoff line at closing, not a wall. Title
  company gets the exact numbers; you walk with what's left, and you'll know that figure
  before you sign anything."
- Discovery: Who's owed, roughly · anyone threatening foreclosure on the lien (HOA can!
  → script 11 gates apply) · is the total under the property's worth?
- Honest exit: if liens drown the equity — "I can't make this work, and anyone who says
  they can is lying. Shortsale with an agent is the honest route; want a name?"

## 24 — Expired / Withdrawn Listing (the S10 channel, owner direct)
- Opener: "Saw the listing came and went — months of showings and nothing to show for it.
  I'm the opposite of relisting: no showings, no contingencies, a number this week."
- Discovery: What killed it — price, condition, feedback? · agent contract expired?
  (⚠️ if still under listing contract → the AGENT scripts 02/03, never around them) ·
  what would you have said yes to?
- Key line (Tyson channel): "The market already told you its retail answer. I'm the
  wholesale answer — lower, but real, and it actually closes."

## 25 — Underwater / Negative Equity (the honest NO script)
- When the math can't work: say so fast, kindly, and leave a gift.
- Line: "Cash buyers like me can't help when more is owed than it's worth — and anyone
  who says otherwise, hold your wallet. The real tools are a shortsale with an agent or a
  workout with the lender. Want the name of an agent who does these?"
- WHY this script exists: honest NOs are where referrals come from. Log every one.

# PART B — BUYER SCRIPTS BY MOTIVE (cash-offer dispositions)

## B1 — Luxury Builder (teardown) — core channel, see 01 + 09
One-liner refresh: "Ready-to-go dirt in your buy-box, under contract, before it hits
market. You see the address after the NCND." (Control-Before-Disclosure, always.)

## B2 — Small Flipper
- Pitch spine: ARV − repairs − their profit = their buy. Lead with the SPREAD, not the house:
  "Comps at $X fixed, needs roughly $Y, available at $Z — that's a $[spread] project 10
  minutes from your last flip."
- They buy certainty of numbers: hand them comps + repair scope honestly; a flipper burned
  once never answers again.

## B3 — Buy-and-Hold Landlord
- Pitch spine: rent + rent-to-price ratio first. "Rents at $2,100, yours at $190K — do
  your own math on the 1%." Tenant-in-place = FEATURE here (day-one income).

## B4 — Developer / Assemblage
- Pitch spine: adjacency + zoning math. "You own/built at [X] — the parcel beside it just
  became available. Together they do what neither does alone." (Monroe model: combined
  R2-7 lots.)

## B5 — The Neighbor
- Often the HIGHEST payer; call them before anyone when a lot is odd-shaped or landlocked.
- Pitch: "Only call like this I'll make — the lot behind your fence is for sale. Owning it
  means privacy forever, and nobody builds next to you. Interested before investors see it?"

## B6 — Out-of-Box Lot Buyer (Norton-type flat fee)
- For lots that FAIL our buy-box (wrong area/price band/problems). Flat-fee lot buyers pay
  ~fixed per accepted lot. Worth $0 otherwise → worth something. (buyers.md Tier 4; test
  with a throwaway first.)

## B7 — Hedge Fund / Institutional (exploration lane)
- 2026 reality: institutional SFH buying is down hard; pitch only STABILIZED, rented,
  newer SFH in their published buy-boxes. Never pitch teardowns here. Lane stays
  exploratory per 08-29 ruling.

## B8 — Builder's Listing Agent (back-channel, from 10-15 dispo cadence)
- Pitch to the agent who lists a builder's finished homes: "Your builder client needs
  dirt to keep you in listings. I source their next three — introduce me, you list what
  they build on it." Their commission motive works FOR us.

## B9 — Proven Cash Buyer (public record)
- Built from recent all-cash purchases in target zips (the S1 upgrade): "County records
  show you closed [address] in cash in March — I source exactly that profile of deal.
  What would make the next one a yes in 10 seconds?"
