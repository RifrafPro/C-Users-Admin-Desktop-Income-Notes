# Project: Income Notes (mortgage-note investing)

## What it is
Investing in mortgage notes ("paper") for income and profit. Core plays:
- **Performing notes** — steady income, bought at/near face value.
- **Non-performing notes** — bought at a discount; work them out (modify,
  re-perform) or take collateral via foreclosure.
- **Partials** — buy a slice of a note's payment stream.
- **Rehabbing paper** — buy NPN cheap → modify with borrower → resell the
  re-performing note at a markup.
