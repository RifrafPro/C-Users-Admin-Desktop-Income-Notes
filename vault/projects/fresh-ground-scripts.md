## 01 — Buyer Intake (builders)  [builder list + numbers in wholesaling.md]
Get on builder acquisition lists + capture pricing inputs. Two ★ answers (BUILD
COST $/sqft, TARGET MARGIN %) calibrate pricing; ⚡ (assignment vs buy-direct) tells
cash needs. Builders: Paramount ⭐, Zimmermann Homes, Sekas Homes, LUKA.

## 02 — On-Market Agent Discovery Call (15 steps)
Call the moment you spot a distressed/aged/price-cut listing. Speed wins.
1 Open (Fresh Ground, re: [address]). 2 **Ask early: "would you represent me as
your buyer?"** (agent earns both sides ~$12k; ~80% yes → they want your offer to win).
3 Seller motivation. 4 Condition (let them talk it down = leverage). 5 Major issues.
6 Upgrades. 7 Special features. 8 Their ARV. 9 Competition (# offers, written/verbal,
homeowner/investor). 10 **THE PRICE: "price to get under contract today?" → "how
flexible?" → "review less?"** + silence (75%+ sell below list). 11 Terms. 12 Timing
(calendar; follow up daily + 1hr before). 13 Other buyers? 14 **Deal extraction:
"other fixer properties, even off-market you could represent me on? buying 5 more this
month."** 15 Wrap (justified offer, get back same day).

## 03 — Agent Close Call + Objections
Call back 15–30 min later, same day. Never just email the offer.
Offer: all-cash, 7-day inspection, 14-day close, $___ → **STOP TALKING.**
- "Too low" → "an offer in beats no offer; start and work toward mutual benefit."
- "Higher offers" → "highest isn't best; they renegotiate at inspection (40–50% fall
  out); be the reliable backup, in writing."
- "Highest & best" → "may not be highest, but I can be best; long-term relationship."
Lock: purchaser name, EMD, **7-day inspection (NEVER omit — your back-out)**, 14-day
close, confirm email, micro-commit ("what time will you send it back?"), POF attached.

## 04 — Off-Market Seller Call (Luxury / Absentee)
NOT distressed — trusts, long-tenure, out-of-state. Peer-to-peer, low pressure; they
buy discretion/certainty/no-hassle, not a discount. "I buy building lots in [area] to
develop… ever thought about selling?" Cash, as-is, their schedule, no listing/showings,
privacy. Let them name the number, go quiet. Discovery: tenure, occupancy, title/
decision-makers, liens/back taxes, timeline, price/best price. Never counter first call.

## 05 — Probate Call (Executor / Heir)
Empathy first ("sorry for your loss"). Executors pick the EASIEST buyer. As-is cash,
no cleanout/repairs/showings, close when estate ready. Differentiator: "take what's
meaningful, leave the rest — I'll handle it." Discovery: location (often out of state),
heirs/decision-maker, probate status/attorney, occupancy, timeline pressure, contents.

## 06 — Seller Negotiation (Getting Under Your Number)
MAO = ceiling. Anchor below MAO, work up only if needed. **Hold technique:** "let me
put you on hold… [30–60s]… talked with my partner, here's what we can do" (third party
to negotiate against). Restate benefits each time. **Close for commitment BEFORE giving
the number:** "if we put something together at that price, can you move forward this
week?" Never negotiate against yourself. At/under MAO → take it.

## 07 — Text Messages
Missed-call text-back within 1 min. Seller sequence (stops when they reply): Day 0 intro
· Day 2 no-pressure · Day 5 social proof (inherited/tired of upkeep) · Day 9 last note.
**Compliance: A2P/10DLC registration required, honor STOP immediately, DNC caution.**

## 08 — Direct Mail
A. Teardown/absentee (luxury, discreet): local building-lot buyer, as-is/all-cash/no
commissions/no showings/discretion; quality stock, hand-addressed. B. Probate/inherited
(empathetic): condolences, simple as-is cash, no cleanout, close on estate timeline.

## 09 — Buyer Blast / Disposition
**Ninja tip: quote price with your fee built in** (under contract $480k → present $500k).
**Always send to 3–5 buyers, never one** (leverage + backup). Lot email: address, lot
sqft, existing structure, finished comps, all-in price, close/clear title — "first to
confirm with POF locks it." SMS + Day-2 nudge ("want it, or should I pass it on?").
