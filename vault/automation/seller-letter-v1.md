Hi {FirstName},

My name is Rich Fabiani — I'm a local real estate investor here in the DC area, not an
agent, and this isn't a listing pitch.

I'm reaching out about your property at {PropertyAddress}. I work with several custom

If you've ever thought about selling — even someday — I'd love a short conversation.
A few things that make working with me different:

• I buy as-is. No cleanup, no repairs, no showings, no open houses.
• No agent commissions — what we agree on is what you receive.
• I can be flexible on timing: fast close if you want out, or months if you need time.
• If the property has complications (taxes owed, family ownership, title issues),
  that's my specialty — I handle problems, not just easy sales.

I'll be honest with you up front: I'm an investor, so I won't be the highest number a
perfect retail sale might fetch. What I offer instead is certainty — a real closing,
no strings, no surprises, and zero cost to you.

Call or text me directly: {RichPhone}. Even if it's just to ask what your property
might be worth to a builder, I'm happy to tell you what I know.

Rich Fabiani
FRESH GROUND — {RichPhone}
"Any property. Fresh start."
