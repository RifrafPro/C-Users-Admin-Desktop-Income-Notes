## Decision tree
- **Title CLEAN (S4) + seller ready** → assignable PSA (our template; "Rich Fabiani, an
  individual, d/b/a FRESH GROUND, and/or assigns"). Earnest ~1% (symbolic is legal; 1%
  reads better). Inspection period = our safety valve.
- **Title MESSY-PRICEABLE** → **OPTION contract first**: $100 option fee, 10-60-day option
  + 5-10-day close (≈90 days control). Walk free if it's rotten. Convert to PSA when cured
  path is confirmed.
- **Multiple heirs** → separate confidential contract per interest (silo protocol).
- **Tax-delinquent** → structure payoffs AT CLOSING from end-buyer funds where possible
  (cuts our cash-in).
- **Assignment to builder** → disclose the partner/fee plainly to the seller ("nothing
  changes on your end; I'm transparent that I earn well if I solve this").
