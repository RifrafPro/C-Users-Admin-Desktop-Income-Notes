## Conversation rules (Fullmer-merged)
1. **Open low & calm, uncertain, conversational** — "…saw your name attached to the
   property on ___, wasn't sure I had the right person. Mind if I ask a couple questions?"
2. Seller talks 70%. Curiosity, not interrogation.
3. **Magic question: "Why haven't y'all sold it before now?"** → the story = the path.
4. Diagnose their problem TO them ("still in your dad's name → that's why title's tied up →
   here's what it takes").
5. **Honest lowball frame:** "I won't be your highest offer — maybe your lowest. But I'm
   the one who can actually close. Retail? Not your guy. Out of the mess? I'm your guy."
   Disclose we profit and why that's fair (nobody else can buy the mess).
6. Under-promise timeline, then over-deliver. Never promise a close date before title recon.
7. People, not leads. Heavy-distress sellers get patience, not pressure.
8. **Heir silos:** feuding co-owners are worked one-by-one, separate confidential deals.
   "Don't punish me for your family's deeds — I'm the new guy with the white flag."
