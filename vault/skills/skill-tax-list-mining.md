# S2 — Delinquent-Tax-List Mining (agent: lead-intake) — STAGE 1, weekly batch

**Strategy (Fullmer):** owners 2–3+ years behind on property taxes are the motivated-seller
flare. Free public records; nobody else is calling them. ~30 filtered leads ≈ 1 deal.
**Source docs:** research/guides/distressed-property-secrets-logan-fullmer.md · wholesaling.md §Fullmer

## Procedure
1. **Pull lists** (per market): Fairfax County VA + Arlington County VA + Falls Church City
   treasurer/tax-administration delinquency records (public; download or FOIA-style request).
   Expansion: Montgomery County MD.
2. **Filter** — keep only: ≥2 years delinquent · high balance · long tenure (12+ yrs) ·
   out-of-state/absentee owner · age signals (estate likely).
3. **Cross-reference against buy-boxes (S1):** keep only parcels matching a confirmed builder
   box — lot size, submarket, zoning. (This is OUR twist: Fullmer buys everything; we snipe
   teardown lots we can pre-sell.)
4. **Skip trace** the keepers: county records → Google/Facebook/obituaries → paid tools if
   needed (Skip Genie tier to start; TLOxp-grade later). No contact yet — Rule: no outreach
   without Rich's send-it.
5. **Create a Deal Record per keeper** (vault/deals/_TEMPLATE.md): parcel, owner, delinquency
   years/amount, matched buyer box, contact info, distress notes.
6. Hand batch to underwriter (S4/S5) ranked by box-fit + distress depth.

## KPI: 30 filtered leads/week → 1-2 offers. Log yield per county to tune filters.
