## THE SCRIPT

**Opener**
> "Hi — Rich Fabiani, I run FRESH GROUND, a land acquisition outfit here in Northern Virginia.
> We source off-market teardown lots and building lots and get them to builders before they
> ever hit the market. Do you have five minutes, or is there someone who handles lot acquisition?"

**⭐ The Vienna-specific hook — use it, it's why they don't hang up:**
> "I'm focused on Vienna right now. I saw you've got [619 Truman / Mashie Ct / your Build-On-Your-
> Lot program] going — so you're clearly active here. That's exactly why I called."

**If "what are you selling?"**
> "Nothing today — that's the point. I only bring lots that fit exactly what a builder wants.
> So I'm calling to learn what YOU want, and you only hear from me again when I'm holding one."

### The 7 questions
1. "Which parts of Vienna are you actively buying in right now — 22180, 22181, 22182?"
2. "What's the ideal lot for you — size, and what do you avoid? Slopes, easements, busy roads?"
3. 💰 **"When a lot fits, what's your realistic range? Vienna land is asking $1.3M for a half
   acre on Malcolm Road and just under $2M for an acre on Wesleyan — where do you actually
   have to be for the numbers to work?"** ← **THE NUMBER. Do not leave the call without it.**
   *If they hedge:* "Let me ask it differently — on a house you'd sell at $2.2M, what can the
   dirt cost you?"
4. ★ **"What's your hard build cost per square foot in Vienna right now?"**
   *(Second-most-valuable number. With #3 and #4 we can compute MAO on any Vienna lot forever.)*
5. ⚡ "Do you buy in your own name, or are you comfortable taking an **assignment of contract**?"
6. "How many lots could you absorb this year if I kept bringing them?"
7. "What made your last land purchase a yes — what tipped it?"

**The Vienna-specific probe (Sekas especially, given Build-On-Your-Lot):**
> "Do you have buyers waiting on lots right now? Because if you've got a client who wants Vienna
> and no dirt to put them on, that's the gap I fill."

**The close**
> "Perfect — that's what I needed. When something in Vienna fits that box, you'll hear from me
> with an address, a price and a plat, not a pitch. What's the best email to send it to?"

## OBJECTIONS

**"We source our own lots."**
> "Most good builders do. I'm not replacing that — I'm the overflow. The ones that never hit
> the MLS, or that sat 300 days because the listing was wrong. Costs you nothing to be on the list."

**"We're not buying right now."**
> "Fair enough. What would have to be true for you to buy again — price, rates, absorption?
> I'd rather know your trigger than call you at the wrong time."

**"What's your fee / are you a wholesaler?"**
> "I'm a principal. My margin is built into the price I quote you — you see one number and
> decide if it works. No commission on top, no surprises at settlement."

**"Send me something and I'll look."**
> "Happy to — but I'd be guessing at your box. Give me two minutes on size and price range and
> everything I send after that is actually relevant."
