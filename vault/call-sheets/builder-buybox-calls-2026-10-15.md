## The script (about 3-4 minutes)
**1. Open (10 sec)**
"Hi, this is Rich Fabiani with Fresh Ground. I find teardown and infill lots in Northern Virginia, mostly off-market. Is this a bad time, or do you have two minutes?"
→ If busy: "When's better, tomorrow morning or afternoon?" Book it, hang up.

**2. Hook (10 sec)** Say the hook line from the table, then:
"I'd rather send a few lots that actually fit than blast people. Can I ask you four quick questions so I only bring you what you'd buy?"

**3. The four questions** (one at a time; let them talk)
1. **Where?** "Which neighborhoods or ZIPs are you buying in right now? Anywhere you're NOT buying?"
2. **What lot?** "Minimum lot size or width that works? Corner lots OK? Anything that kills a lot for you: slope, RPA, septic?"
3. **What price?** "What are you paying for a lot in [their area] these days? Or how do you back into it, a percent of the finished price?"
   - Silence is fine. If they dodge, give a number from the records and let them correct it: "I'm seeing builders pay around [$1.1M in 22207 / $600-700K in South Arlington R2-7]. Is that about right for you?"
4. **How?** "Are you OK buying by assignment of contract? What feasibility period and close timeline do you like? Any title company you prefer?"

**4. Pipeline question (Tyson Smith rule: builders who are short on lots overpay)**
"How's your pipeline? Are you short on lots for next year, or pretty full?"

**5. Close (15 sec)**
"Perfect. When I have something that fits, I'll send you a blind summary first: area, size, price. If you like it, I'll send a short non-circumvention agreement, then the full package with survey, zoning and title status. What's the best email for that, and is it you who decides?"

## If they ask…
- **"What do you have right now?"** → "A couple of things in Arlington and Great Falls I'm still tying up. I don't share addresses until they're under contract. I'll send you the blind summary as soon as they are." (True. Never name Monroe.)
- **"Who are you? How many deals have you done?"** → "I'm newer to lots. My edge is the homework: I check zoning, title and utilities before I bring you anything, so you don't waste a feasibility period." (Honest. Never claim deals we haven't done.)
- **"Just send me everything."** → "I will, but only what fits your box. That's why I'm asking."
- **"We don't buy through wholesalers."** → "Understood. Can I ask what would make it work? Some builders only want deals with a zoning letter and a survey already done." Log it, move on.
