## 🛡️ Objections
- **"We source our own / have a Realtor."** → "Perfect — I'm one more pipeline of the *off-market*
  ones that never hit MLS. Costs nothing; you only look when one fits your box."
- **"Just email me."** → "Will do — best direct email? And quick, so my first fits: sweet-spot lot
  size + top neighborhood?"
- **"What's your fee?"** → "Built into the lot price I quote. You like the number or you don't. No
  separate fee."
