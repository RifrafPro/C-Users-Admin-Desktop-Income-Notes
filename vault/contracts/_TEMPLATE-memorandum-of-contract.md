## ⚠️ READ BEFORE USING — this instrument has teeth in both directions

**What it does:** recording a Memorandum puts the world on notice that you hold an equitable
interest under a purchase contract. A title search will find it. In practice it means the seller
cannot quietly sell to someone else and close clean — a title company will require your release.

**What it costs you if misused:**
1. **It clouds the seller's title.** If your contract terminates and you do not promptly record a
   release, you have left a cloud on someone's property. In Virginia that can expose you to a
   **slander of title** claim and to damages. **Always pair this with the Release below.**
2. **It can sour the relationship.** Recording without telling the seller reads as hostile. Fullmer's
   own framing — "use it wisely" — is about *selective* use.
3. **It is not a lien.** It does not secure money owed. It gives notice of a contract right.

**Fullmer's own rule of thumb: record it when the deal is juicy enough to be worth the friction.**
Our working test — record only when **(a)** the spread is large enough that being cut out is a
material loss, **AND (b)** there is an actual signal of risk: multiple heirs or co-owners, a seller
who has shopped it before, a prior contract that died, or an agent hinting at other interest.
On a clean single-owner deal with a cooperative seller, **do not record — the friction is not worth it.**

**🔴 VIRGINIA CAUTION:** see `research/va-md-assignment-law.md`. Recording a Memorandum is a public,
documentary act evidencing that you are dealing in an assignable real estate contract. If we are
pursuing multiple assignments in a 12-month period, **a recorded trail is exactly what a regulator
would look at.** Discuss with counsel before adopting this as routine practice in VA.

# MEMORANDUM OF CONTRACT

**THIS MEMORANDUM OF CONTRACT** is made and given this ______ day of ____________, 20____,
by and between:

**SELLER:** [FULL LEGAL NAME(S) EXACTLY AS THEY APPEAR ON THE DEED — including trustee
capacity, e.g. "JANE A. DOE, Trustee of the Doe Family Revocable Trust dated 3/20/2018"]

and

**PURCHASER:** Rich Fabiani, an individual, d/b/a FRESH GROUND

## 1. PURPOSE
This Memorandum is executed and recorded for the sole purpose of giving public notice of the
existence of a certain written Purchase and Sale Agreement (the "Agreement") between the parties
named above, affecting the real property described below. **This Memorandum is not a lien and does
not secure the payment of any indebtedness.**

## 2. PROPERTY
The real property that is the subject of the Agreement is located in
**[COUNTY/CITY], [STATE]**, and is more particularly described as:

- **Tax Map / Parcel ID:** [PIN]
- **Address:** [FULL ADDRESS]
- **Legal Description:** [INSERT THE LEGAL DESCRIPTION FROM THE DEED — subdivision name, lot and
  block, section, plat book and page. Do NOT paraphrase; copy it exactly from the recorded deed.]
- **Prior instrument reference:** Deed Book ______, Page ______, among the land records of
  [JURISDICTION]

## 3. DATE OF AGREEMENT
The Agreement was executed by the parties on ______________, 20____.

## 4. INTEREST CREATED
Under the Agreement, Purchaser holds an **equitable interest** in the Property, together with the
right to purchase the Property upon the terms and conditions stated in the Agreement.

## 5. ASSIGNABILITY
The Agreement is **assignable** by Purchaser, in whole or in part, and Purchaser's rights hereunder
inure to the benefit of Purchaser's successors and assigns.

## 6. TERM AND AUTOMATIC EXPIRATION ⭐
This Memorandum shall be effective from the date of recording and shall **automatically expire and
be of no further force or effect on ______________, 20____** [insert a date tied to the outside
closing date under the Agreement, plus a short tail], **without the necessity of any further act
or recording by either party**, unless extended by a written instrument executed by both parties
and recorded prior to that date.

> *Why this clause matters: a self-expiring Memorandum is the single best protection against the
> slander-of-title exposure described above. Never record one without it.*

## 7. RELEASE UPON TERMINATION
Upon termination or expiration of the Agreement for any reason, Purchaser shall, **within five (5)
business days of written request by Seller**, execute and record a release of this Memorandum in
recordable form.

## 8. NO AMENDMENT
This Memorandum does not amend, modify or supersede the Agreement. In the event of any conflict
between this Memorandum and the Agreement, **the Agreement controls.** The full terms of the
Agreement, including price, are intentionally not stated herein and remain confidential between
the parties.

## 9. COUNTERPARTS
This Memorandum may be executed in counterparts, each of which shall be deemed an original.

## ⬜ Before first use
1. **Have a VA real estate attorney review this once.** Memoranda are routine instruments, but
   recording requirements, margin rules and acknowledgment form are jurisdiction-specific, and a
   defective instrument can be rejected at the counter or — worse — recorded and unenforceable.
2. **Confirm Fairfax Circuit Court recording requirements** — current fee, margin, and whether a
   cover sheet is required.
3. **Never record without the §6 expiration date filled in.**
4. Log every recording in the relevant Deal Record, with a diarised date to file the Release.
