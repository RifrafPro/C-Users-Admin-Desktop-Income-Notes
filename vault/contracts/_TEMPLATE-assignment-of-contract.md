## 3. Assignee's Acknowledgment
Assignee acknowledges that Assignee has (a) received and **reviewed the Original Contract**
and all exhibits, (b) conducted or waived its own due diligence on the Property, including
zoning, buildability, and value, and (c) is **not relying on any representation of Assignor**
regarding the Property or the Seller. The Property is taken **AS-IS** per the Original Contract.

## 4. Closing & Deed
Assignee shall perform all Buyer obligations under the Original Contract and close on or
before the closing date stated therein (**[[CLOSING DATE]]**). Title shall convey **directly
from Seller to Assignee** at Closing. Assignee shall deposit any required earnest money and
funds directly with the Settlement Agent: **[[TITLE COMPANY]]**.

## 5. Assignor Representations
Assignor represents: (a) the Original Contract is in full force and has not been amended
except as attached; (b) to Assignor's knowledge, neither Assignor nor Seller is in default;
(c) Assignor has full right and authority to make this Assignment.

## 6. Default & Remedies
- If **Assignee** fails to close (other than due to Seller/Assignor default), Assignor retains
  the non-refundable deposit (§2) and this Assignment terminates; Assignor's other rights are
  reserved.
- If **Assignor** materially defaults, Assignee's deposit under §2 is refunded as its sole
  remedy against Assignor.
- Neither Party is liable for the **Seller's** performance under the Original Contract.

## 7. Indemnification
Assignee shall indemnify and hold Assignor harmless from any obligation, cost, or liability
under the Original Contract arising from and after the Effective Date of this Assignment.
Assignor shall indemnify Assignee for matters arising before it.
