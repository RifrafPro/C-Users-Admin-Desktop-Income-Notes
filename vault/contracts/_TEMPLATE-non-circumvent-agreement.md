This Non-Circumvention & Non-Disclosure Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into by and

**Discloser:** FRESH GROUND, LLC ("FRESH GROUND"), [[ADDRESS]]

(each a "Party," together the "Parties")

## 1. Purpose
FRESH GROUND sources teardown and redevelopment lots and introduces them to a select
group of builders. In connection with this relationship, FRESH GROUND may disclose to
Recipient certain confidential information, including property addresses, seller/owner
identity or contact information, pricing, deal terms, and sourcing methods
(collectively, "Confidential Information").

## 2. Non-Circumvention
Recipient agrees that for any property, seller, or owner introduced or identified by
FRESH GROUND (a "FRESH GROUND Opportunity"), Recipient will NOT, directly or indirectly,
for a period of **[[24]] months** from the date of first disclosure:
- contact, negotiate with, or transact with the seller/owner outside of FRESH GROUND;
- acquire, option, or otherwise obtain rights to the property except through FRESH GROUND
  or with FRESH GROUND's written consent; or
- circumvent FRESH GROUND to avoid paying the assignment fee or compensation FRESH GROUND
  would otherwise earn on that opportunity.

This restriction does NOT apply to any property Recipient can show, by dated written
record, it already knew of or was independently pursuing before FRESH GROUND's disclosure.

## 3. Non-Disclosure
Recipient will keep all Confidential Information confidential and use it solely to
evaluate a FRESH GROUND Opportunity. Recipient will not disclose Confidential Information
to any third party (including other wholesalers, agents, or builders) without FRESH
GROUND's prior written consent.

## 4. Remedies
The Parties agree that a breach of Sections 2 or 3 causes harm difficult to quantify.
If Recipient circumvents FRESH GROUND on a FRESH GROUND Opportunity, Recipient agrees to
pay FRESH GROUND, as liquidated damages (not a penalty), a flat amount of
**Twenty-Five Thousand Dollars ($25,000)** per Opportunity circumvented. This is in
addition to, not in place of, any other legal remedy.

## 5. Term
This Agreement remains in effect for **[[24]] months** from the Effective Date and survives
with respect to any FRESH GROUND Opportunity disclosed during that period, per Section 2.

## 6. Governing Law
This Agreement is governed by the laws of the Commonwealth of Virginia, without regard to
conflict-of-law principles.

## 7. No Partnership
This Agreement does not create a partnership, joint venture, or agency relationship
between the Parties.
