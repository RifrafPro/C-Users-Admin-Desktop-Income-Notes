prospective assignees.

## 6. Feasibility / Inspection Period  ← YOUR EXIT
Buyer shall have **[[10]] calendar days** from the Effective Date ("Feasibility Period") to,
at Buyer's sole discretion and expense, investigate the Property — including title, zoning,
buildability, lot yield, utilities, soils, permits, and value. **Buyer may terminate this
Agreement for any reason or no reason** by written notice on or before the end of the
Feasibility Period, whereupon the EMD shall be **refunded in full** to Buyer and neither
Party shall have further obligation.
