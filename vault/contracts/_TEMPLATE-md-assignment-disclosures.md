## ⚠️ WHY BOTH OF THESE ARE MANDATORY

§ 10-715 requires **two separate written disclosures at two different moments**. Miss either one and
the statute hands the other side a **rescission right without penalty at any time before closing** —
meaning a deal can evaporate the day before settlement, and an assignee gets their deposit back.

| # | Who gives it | Who receives it | **WHEN — this timing is the whole point** |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | Wholesale **buyer** (us) | The **property owner / seller** | **BEFORE entering into the contract of sale** |
| **2** | Wholesale **seller** (us) | The **prospective assignee** (the builder) | **BEFORE assigning** the contract |

**Scope — read this before assuming it applies.** § 10-715 reaches **"residential property"** =
improved by **four or fewer single-family dwelling units** intended for human habitation, and both
statutory definitions refer to **owner-occupied** residential property.
- ✅ **Applies:** our Bethesda / Chevy Chase / Potomac **teardown-house** deals.
- ❓ **Probably outside:** a **vacant lot** is not "improved by dwelling units."
- ⬜ **Confirm with MD counsel** before relying on the carve-out. **When in doubt, disclose** —
  the cost of an unnecessary disclosure is zero; the cost of a missing one is the deal.
- Does **not** apply to foreclosure sales under Md. Rule 14-215.
- **Prospective only** — no effect on contracts executed before Oct 1, 2025.

**NOTICE OF POTENTIAL ASSIGNMENT OF CONTRACT OF SALE**
*Provided pursuant to Maryland Real Property Article § 10-715(c)(1)*

**Property:** [FULL ADDRESS], [COUNTY], Maryland [ZIP]
**Tax Account / Parcel ID:** [ID]
**Prospective Purchaser:** Rich Fabiani, an individual, d/b/a FRESH GROUND
**Date:** ______________, 20____

To the Owner(s) of the above Property:

Before you enter into a contract of sale for the Property, Maryland law requires that I give you
this written notice.

> **I MAY ASSIGN THE CONTRACT OF SALE FOR THIS PROPERTY TO ANOTHER PERSON.**

This means that although I am signing the contract as the purchaser, I may transfer my rights under
that contract to a different buyer, who would then complete the purchase in my place. If I do so, I
may receive a fee for that assignment.

If I assign the contract of sale and **have not** provided you with this notice, Maryland law allows
you to **rescind (cancel) the contract without penalty at any time before closing.**

The terms of your sale — including your purchase price — do not change because of an assignment.

**NOTICE OF EQUITABLE INTEREST AND LIMITATION ON ABILITY TO CONVEY TITLE**
*Provided pursuant to Maryland Real Property Article § 10-715(c)(2)*

**Property:** [FULL ADDRESS], [COUNTY], Maryland [ZIP]
**Tax Account / Parcel ID:** [ID]
**Assignor:** Rich Fabiani, an individual, d/b/a FRESH GROUND
**Prospective Assignee:** [BUILDER LEGAL NAME]
**Date:** ______________, 20____

Before you take an assignment of the contract of sale for the Property, Maryland law requires that
I give you this written notice.

> **I HOLD AN EQUITABLE INTEREST IN THE PROPERTY. I DO NOT HOLD LEGAL TITLE, AND I MAY NOT BE ABLE
> TO CONVEY TITLE TO THE PROPERTY.**

What that means:
1. I am **not** the record owner of the Property. My interest arises solely from a written contract
   of sale with the record owner dated ______________, 20____.
2. What I am assigning to you are **my rights under that contract** — not the Property itself.
3. Completion of the purchase depends on the seller performing under that contract and on clear,
   insurable title being delivered at settlement. **I cannot guarantee either.**
4. You should conduct your own title examination and due diligence.

If I assign this contract and **have not** provided you with this notice, Maryland law allows you to
**rescind the agreement to purchase without penalty at any time before closing**, and you are
**entitled to a refund of any deposit** paid in connection with the assignment.

## ⬜ Before first use
1. **Have a Maryland real estate attorney review both forms once.** The statutory language is
   quoted above, but form, delivery and proof-of-receipt practice should be confirmed.
2. Confirm whether § 10-715 reaches **vacant land** and **non-owner-occupied** property.
3. Wire both disclosures into **S7 (deal structuring)** and **S8 (disposition)** as hard gates —
   the PSA cannot be signed, and the assignment cannot be executed, until the matching disclosure
   is signed and filed.

> 🔴 **Note:** these forms address **Maryland only.** Virginia's issue is entirely different — it is
> a **licensing** question under Va. Code § 54.1-2100(iii), not a disclosure question, and no form
> solves it. See `research/va-md-assignment-law.md`.
