## 4. 🟡 The "Double Dip" / agent-sourcing angle

The free agent report ("How to Get Agents to Bring You Deals") is email-gated, but its own outline
reveals the three components: an **Agent Offer Sheet** (10 things agents must include when writing your
offers), **phone/email/SMS scripts** for working with agents, and the **"Double Dip Technique"** — a way
to motivate agents to bring you deals *before* they're listed.

The mechanic behind "double dip" is standard and needs no PDF: **let the listing agent represent both
sides.** An agent who brings you an off-market pocket listing and writes your offer earns both
commissions, which is exactly why they'd call you first. Norton's webinar also names agents as one of
only **two** lot-sourcing methods (the other being software).

**Relevance is immediate:** our 08-28 sweep produced a list of *agent-listed* stale lots and their
direct phone numbers. That is an agent-relationship channel we have not worked. See S11 §4.

## 6. Funnel mechanics — for the record

Worth naming so we recognise the pattern next time. The site is a textbook value-ladder: ~40 free
email-gated "tools/reports" → evergreen auto-webinars on **joinnow.live** (fake-live: today's date,
synthetic attendee roster, Norton listed as an attendee in his own webinar) → $1,000 programs →
Inner Circle coaching → live events. Their own **disclaimer page** states: *"Consumers who use Our
products can generally expect not to see any increase in sales, leads, profits, influence, or positive
results of any kind."*

That single line is the most honest thing on the site, and it's the correct lens for all of it.
**This is Norton touchpoint #4 in the vault** (see `courses-funnels.md`, `deal-analysis-tools.md`,
`research-email-findings.md`). Standing rule holds: **buy nothing; take the method, skip the ladder.**
