SECTION 1 
Seller Outreach: Initial SMS Your opening text needs to be short, human-sounding, and non-threatening. No walls of text. No company names. No links. Just a simple question from one person to another.   Script A (Direct) Hi [First Name], I came across your lot at [Address] in [County]. Would you consider selling? We buy land as-is, cash, close in 30 days. - Caleb   Script B (Soft question) Hey [First Name], saw you own some land in [County]. Not sure if you've thought about selling, but we'd love to make you a quick cash offer if you're open to it. Worth a 2-min chat?   Script C (High-equity counties) Hi [First Name], looking to buy land near [City] area. Saw you own a lot on [Street]. Any chance you'd take a cash offer?  Rotate between A, B, and C across different campaigns to avoid response pattern fatigue. If one pulls more replies in a specific county, stick with it.

The 3-Touch Follow-Up Sequence Most deals come from the 2nd or 3rd touch, not the first. Set these up as an automated drip in SmarterContact before you launch.   Day 3 Follow-Up Hey [First Name], just wanted to follow up on the land at [Address]. Still happy to make you a cash offer if you're open to it. No pressure either way.   Day 7 Follow-Up Hi [First Name], last follow-up from me on [Address]. If you ever decide to sell, I can have a number to you within 24 hours. Just reply whenever.   Day 14 Final Touch [First Name], going to close out my file on [Address]. If the timing ever gets right, I'm still interested. Take care.  After Day 14, move the contact to your retargeting list. Run them again in 90 days. Circumstances change. A seller who said no in April often says yes in August.

SECTION 4 
Making and Delivering the Offer Once you've pulled comps and have a number, here's how to present it. Keep it clean, confident, and easy to say yes to.   Offer Delivery (Text) Hi [First Name], I did some digging on [Address]. Based on comparable sales nearby, I can offer you $[AMOUNT] cash, as-is, closing in 30 days through a licensed title company. No fees, no commissions. Would that work for you?   Offer Delivery (Email) Subject: Cash Offer - [Address]  Hi [First Name],  Following up on our conversation about [Address]. After reviewing comparable sales in the area, I'd like to offer $[AMOUNT] for the property.  Terms: Cash purchase, as-is condition, 30-day close through a licensed title company. No fees or commissions on your end.  If this works, I can have the contract to you today via DocuSign. Just reply here or call/text me at [Phone].  Thanks, Caleb Milobsky Elevate Real Estate Partners   Counter Response I appreciate the counter. Let me see if there's any room to move. Give me a few hours to revisit the comps and I'll come back to you.
