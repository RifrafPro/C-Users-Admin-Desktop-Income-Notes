**His model vs ours.** He runs **cold SMS** to skip-traced owners in cheap rural counties: lots at $20-200K, 18,000 texts per deal.
Ours is luxury teardown lots in NoVA at $400K-1.2M, consent-first, with builders as the exit. Take the tools, not the channel.

## ⛔ Do NOT adopt
- **Cold SMS to skip-traced numbers** (Ch. 5, Script Vault §1-2). It breaks our consent-first rule. The TCPA/A2P risk is ours, not his.
  His 16-17% "response rate" is cold texting. Our substitutes are letters (D3), listing-agent email, and inbound calls.
- His market criteria (lots at $20-200K, "avoid $500K+"). Our lots sit above that band by design; builders are the reason.
