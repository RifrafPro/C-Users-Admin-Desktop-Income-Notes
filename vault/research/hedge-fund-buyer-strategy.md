## The model (how it worked)
Institutional SFR buyers (Invitation Homes, Progress Residential, AMH, Tricon, FirstKey,
Amherst…) underwrite to RENTAL YIELD, not comps — so in the ZIRP era (2020-21) they paid
at/near retail for anything fitting the box: 3bd/2ba SFH, 1990s+, $200-450K, rental
suburbs, light repairs, vacant. Wholesalers assigned at tiny seller discounts. "Reverse
wholesaling to hedge funds" = get the fund's buy box FIRST, then源 source to it — literally
our FRESH GROUND spine with a different buyer.
