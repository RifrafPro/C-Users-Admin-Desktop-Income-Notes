# NORTH STAR — The National Deal Engine (Rich's ruling, 2026-10-16)

**Rich's words:** "An automated workflow to find, evaluate and create leads for this type
of property, covering the entire country. Not looking for specific $ deals — quick,
high-%, easy, profitable, no out-of-pocket expense."

## The funnel (every stage already has a built or building component)
1. **FIND (national, ~$0):** Propwire national filters (17 lead types, 105 keywords,
   saved-search alerts) · expired-listing channel (S10/24) · county tax rolls (S2) ·
   Land Insights where it adds. Target signals = the routing table in
   fresh-ground-scripts-situational.md.
2. **TRIAGE (pennies):** Jev classifies every raw lead (~$0.0004 each: fit / not / unsure,
   distress type → script number). Humans never read raw firehose.
3. **EVALUATE (autonomous):** underwriter agent — county records (S4, proven on Chain
   Bridge), as-is desktop valuation (no ARV religion), the learned land-ratio bands,
   lien math. Output: spread %, hair level, GO/NO-GO.
4. **CREATE THE LEAD (the scripts):** email-first opt-in (consent logged in Mission
   Control) → voice agent discovery call (scripts 11-25, qualify per current autonomy
   ruling) → escalation packet lands in front of Rich with the one question to ask.
5. **RICH GATE:** price/contract decisions (until carve-out #1 activates per market).
6. **CONTRACT ($100 option / low EMD):** the no-out-of-pocket mechanism — control without
   capital (Fullmer model, already policy).
7. **DISPO (B1-B9):** buyer matched by motive; fee collected at close via title company.

## The filter that defines "quick high-% easy" (machine-checkable)
A lead is QUICK-WIN only if ALL true:
- Spread ≥ 25% of resale value after every payoff (liens/taxes come out of price, not pocket)
- Title hair ≤ 1 issue the title company calls routine (no open bankruptcy, no litigation)
- One decision-maker reachable (or clean executor) — no feuding-heir webs at national scale
- A buyer CHANNEL already exists in that metro (see expansion rule)
- Legal lane is green for that state + situation (see legal map)
Everything else: nurture queue or honest-NO (script 25). The engine's edge is saying no fast.

## The two honest physics problems national scale must respect
1. **Deals die at dispo, not at intake.** A contract with no buyer is a liability.
   **EXPANSION RULE: we enter a market buyer-first** — B9 (proven cash buyers from county
   records) builds the metro's buyer list BEFORE lead-gen turns on there. No buyers
   mapped = market stays dark. This is the single rule that keeps "national" from
   becoming "nationally overextended."
2. **50 states = 50 rulebooks.** Assignment/wholesaling and distressed-owner laws vary
   by state (we have VA/MD verified; PHIFA-type laws exist elsewhere). **LEGAL MAP
   requirement:** a state unlocks in the engine only when its one-page rules file exists
   in vault/research/legal-map/ (what's allowed, what's gated, license triggers).
   Build order: VA ✅ MD ✅ DC → FL → AZ → then by buyer density.

## Phase gates (each phase must COMPLETE A PAID DEAL before the next unlocks)
- **P1 — Prove the loop (NOW):** NoVA. Monroe offer → negotiation → contract → dispo →
  fee wired. The national engine is this loop with more zip codes; it cannot clone a
  loop that has never closed.
- **P2 — Three-metro engine:** DC + S.FL + PHX (briefs exist since 08-29). Email-first
  funnels, Jev triage at volume, A2P registration done (gates texting), voice agent
  carve-out activated per Rich.
- **P3 — National:** state legal map grows buyer-first; Hermes front desk handles inbound
  at all hours; Rich's role = gates 5 and 7 only. The 12%+ regional profit model prices
  every market's MAO automatically.

## No-out-of-pocket ledger (what the engine spends vs. never spends)
SPENDS (pennies-to-dollars): data mostly $0 · Jev ~$0.40/1k leads · voice ~$0.10/min ·
email $0 · $100 option per controlled deal (refundable-risk capital, first fee replenishes all).
